06 · Questions we get

Honest answers,
no hedging.

If you don't see your question here, ask. We answer in plain language — the audit conversation usually clears the rest up.

Do we have to switch off our current PMS?+

No. Acel Cortex sits on top of what you already use — AppFolio, Yardi, Buildium, RentManager, whatever. We integrate via API or watcher, depending on what your system supports. The goal is to make your existing stack quieter, not to replace it.

How are the agents kept from going off-script?+

Every agent ships with a written policy, an explicit list of allowed actions, and a human-in-the-loop gate for anything financial, legal, or irreversible. We log every decision the agent makes and every message it sends — readable like an email thread, not a debug trace.

What does pricing look like, actually?+

The audit is $0. Standard automations are a flat per-workflow build fee plus a per-seat monthly. Custom builds are scoped and quoted up front — no surprise hourly. We don't charge platform fees and we don't take a cut of recovered revenue.

Who owns the data and the configuration?+

You do. All tenant communications, ledger data, and audit logs live in your tenant. The agent configuration — prompts, rules, allowed actions — is exported on request as plain JSON. If we part ways, you walk away with everything we built together.

How small a portfolio is too small?+

If you have at least one full-time operator drowning in inbound, the math usually works at 150 units and up. Below that, we'll tell you — and recommend a couple of off-the-shelf tools instead of trying to sell you a build.

Are you only for property management?+

Today, mostly. Property managers are where the playbook is sharpest. We've also shipped audits for adjacent operators — construction, brokerage back-office, multi-family insurance. If your business runs on inbound + tickets + ledgers, the pattern usually transfers.
