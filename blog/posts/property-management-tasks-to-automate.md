Whether you manage your own rentals or other people's, you are probably doing at least seven things every week that AI can do for you around the clock. Here is the list I use at our 800-unit property management company, in the order I would hand them off.

## 1. Answering tenant messages

Almost every tenant message falls into one of three buckets: the lease (what they can and cannot do), their balance, or maintenance. Connect AI to your inbox, let it learn how you answer each type, and it can handle **80 to 90 percent** of tenant communication. You step in for anything outside that scope.

## 2. Answering tenant phone calls

If you are still picking up every tenant call yourself, that is a problem on its own. Calls interrupt your day, and after-hours calls are how property managers and landlords burn out.

Two fixes, in order:

- Train tenants to use email or your property management software's portal instead of calling.
- For tenants who still call, put an AI receptionist on the line 24/7 as a buffer.

It takes a one-time setup and onboarding, and it gives you your evenings back.

## 3. Sending notices of entry

Logging in to draft a notice of entry for pest control or a repair is pure busywork. Now I tell the AI the property, the time and the reason. It pulls the details from our property management software, fills in the template and asks me to approve. Once I approve, it sends the notice, updates the system and sends the tenant a reminder.

## 4. Processing invoices

If you own a few properties, entering invoices is easy. If you manage for other owners, every invoice means reading it, uploading it, updating the task and entering charges.

Now every invoice goes straight into the bills section of our property management software, and the AI asks me only one thing: what to charge on the owner's statement. A bookkeeper reviews everything weekly. The result is no more manual invoice entry, and no missed invoices.

## 5. Comparing move-in and move-out inspections

At turnover, someone has to compare the move-in and move-out reports and decide what is normal wear and what the tenant owes. It is slow, tedious work.

For the past year our team has not done it by hand. We upload both reports, the AI compares them, lists what changed and gives a first take on what is reasonable to charge. We get a clear explanation for the tenant, fewer disputes, and a professional report to share with the owner.

## 6. Scheduling vendors

Once 1 to 5 are running, this is the next step. Maintenance requests fall into categories, and you already have preferred vendors for each. The AI gathers details and photos from the tenant, contacts the right vendor, confirms a time and asks you to approve. Then it puts the job on your calendar and sends the tenant a notice of entry.

With this in place, **70 to 80 percent** of maintenance requests no longer pass through your hands. You can read how we set this up in [How to Automate Repair Scheduling in Property Management](/blog/automate-repair-scheduling).

## 7. Booking showings

This is the most tedious task, and also the one that decides whether you make money. A vacant unit bleeds cash whether it is yours or an owner's. An AI leasing assistant connected to your listings, including Facebook Marketplace, can answer prospects, check basic qualification and book showings.

Imagine logging off at 6 p.m. and seeing tomorrow's showings booked by 9.

## Where to start

You built a company or a portfolio to own something, not to give yourself a job. None of these seven tasks requires you personally. Pick one this week. If you are not sure which, start with tenant messages, because it frees the most time with the least risk.

## FAQ

### Which task should I automate first?

Tenant messages. They take the most hours, they are easy to sort into a few buckets, and you can keep a person on anything unusual while the AI learns.

### Will tenants be annoyed talking to AI?

Most tenants care about a fast, correct answer. AI replies in minutes at any hour, and anything outside its scope still goes to a person. Tenants who want a human still get one.

### Does this work with my property management software?

Yes. Acel Cortex works on top of Buildium, AppFolio, Rentvine, Rent Manager and other major platforms, with no migration. Your software stays the system of record, and the AI works alongside it.

### Is it worth automating if I only have a few rentals?

Tasks 1, 2 and 7 pay off at any size because they protect your time and fill vacancies faster. Invoices, inspections and vendor scheduling pay off most once you manage for other owners or have more than a handful of units.
