    How it changes the work

    Your team becomes the second layer.

    Today your admins are the first to touch every invoice, email and request. With Acel Cortex they review work that is already done, and spend their time on the exceptions that need judgment. So you can take on new doors without the back office breaking.

    Layer 1 · Acel Cortex

    Reads, sorts and drafts

    • Pulls invoices, emails and requests from every inbox
    • Matches, codes and checks for duplicates
    • Triages maintenance and drafts replies
    • Prepares notices, assessments and owner updates
    Layer 2 · Your team

    Reviews and approves

    • Checks the batch, line by line, on one screen
